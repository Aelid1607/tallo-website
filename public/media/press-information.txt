TALLO PRESS INFORMATION
Updated 27 July 2026

SHORT DESCRIPTION

Tallo is a free Australian shopping app that compares grocery and liquor
prices, prices complete shopping lists, plans meals and helps households make
more informed weekly-shopping decisions.

FULL DESCRIPTION

Tallo is a free Australian shopping app built to make the weekly shop easier
to plan and compare. It brings grocery prices from Coles, Woolworths and Aldi,
plus liquor prices from BWS, Liquorland and Dan Murphy's, into one practical
experience. People can build and share shopping lists, compare complete store
totals, use Multi-shop to review a lower-cost split, scan product barcodes,
plan meals, browse or create recipes, import supported recipe links, view
nearby fuel prices and track completed shops and savings.

FOUNDER

Brett Hevers is the Founder and Director of Tallo Pty Ltd. A father of two and
owner of two dogs, he spent ten years running a home-services business before
building Tallo. The original idea came from trying to verify a supermarket
special in a store with patchy reception. What began as a simple A-or-B price
comparison grew into an all-in-one shopping, meal-planning, recipe and fuel
app.
